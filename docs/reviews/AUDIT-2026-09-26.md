## P0 — production, fix now (independent of the preview)

### P0-1 Shared demo password for all demo accounts, including PLATFORM ADMIN (security)
- Evidence:
  - `DataSeeder.java` hardcodes one shared password for the demo accounts, including `admin@vikisol.dev` (**platform admin**) and the company admin.
  - It **logs those passwords at startup** (lines 154–158).
  - `SEED_ENABLED` **defaults to `true`** (`application.yml` line 131). Production demo accounts exist (the tests sign in with them against the production API).
  - The public sign-in page even offers a **"Platform admin"** role chip.
  - Admin 2FA is optional (`SECURITY-FINDINGS.md`).
- Risk: anyone who has seen the repo or the logs can sign in as platform admin on production.
- **Fix:**
  - set `SEED_ENABLED=false` in production;
  - **change the passwords of every demo account in production**, and disable or delete the platform-admin demo account;
  - create the real platform admin with a strong password, and **turn on `ADMIN_2FA_REQUIRED` for PLATFORM_ADMIN now**;
  - move demo passwords to env-only for local/test, and delete the log lines;
  - remove the Recruiter / Hiring manager / Platform admin chips from the public sign-in page (internal roles log in without a public selector);
  - tests read demo credentials from a gitignored env or CI secrets.
  - Never print the credentials in any report.

### P0-2 Honesty: public claims include seeded fake people, bids and real brand names
- Production landing says "**all real people nearby**", "**79 people open to work**", "Every bid shows a **real match percentage**". Preview says "The count is the real one" and shows a **LIVE BID** with "94% match".
- `LandingService` counts `searchableByEnterprises` profiles and the featured bid **without excluding seeded demo data**. The seeder creates **40 fake candidates**, marketplace bids with match scores, and companies.
- Seeded companies use **real brand names** (e.g. "Swiggy", "Techolution") with invented roles, industries and projects. That's an impersonation and legal risk.
- **Policy (architect decision):**
  - (a) **now:** public totals, the featured bid and "live" claims **exclude demo data**; if nothing real remains, the sentence shows without a number;
  - every seeded company is renamed to an obviously **fictional** name;
  - every demo item carries the DEMO badge everywhere it appears;
  - (b) **before the Gachibowli launch:** remove all demo content from production (an admin-only cleanup, not a destructive migration).
